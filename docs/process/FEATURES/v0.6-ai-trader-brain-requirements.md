# Feature requirements: v0.6-ai-trader-brain

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Waldemar  
**Date:** 2026-08-20  

## Goal

Replace the current net-worth-only AI scorers with one shared **trader brain**. Three seats still differ only by **weights**: Aggressive, Middle, and Defensive. The brain judges **risk vs potential (chance)** — especially that low prices are both dangerous and high-leverage — and prefers **share leads in companies where the hand is strong**.

## In scope

- One shared evaluation model for all AI seats (same features, same search/lookahead shape)
- Three strategies as weight profiles: `aggressive`, `middle`, `defensive` (UI labels: Aggressive / Middle / Defensive)
- Rename/retire `wealth` / `punish` / `balanced` (Investor / Raider / Mixed) in types, setup, UI, rules text, and tests
- Score terms for **chance (potential)** and **risk**, plus light anchors for own wealth and lead vs best rival
- Trade and card decisions that stop common failure modes: long cash-only stretches, empty card plays with no position, and careless all-in into wipeout without hand edge
- Engine unit tests for the new contracts (determinism + strategy differentiation + key trader behaviors)
- Setup defaults: one AI seat per profile (with one human), same 2–4 seat rules as today

## Out of scope

- LLM / ML / learned policies
- Peeking at face-down draw order
- New difficulty levels beyond the three weight profiles
- Changing card catalog, split/wipeout thresholds, starting cash/hand, or round rules
- Perfect Monte Carlo over the secret pile
- Teaching the AI full multi-turn opponent modeling beyond public share counts and current hand

## Users / context

Hotseat solo / mixed games where AI should feel like a human trader: build relative position where cards are good, and size low-price bets by risk vs potential — not pure cash turtling or random all-ins.

## Decisions (proposed — lock on agree)

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Architecture | **One brain**, three **weight vectors**; no separate ad-hoc policy trees per seat |
| Strategy ids / labels | `aggressive` → **Aggressive**; `middle` → **Middle**; `defensive` → **Defensive** |
| Migration | Old ids removed (no silent alias forever); setup/defaults/tests updated in this slice |
| Chance | Upside / potential: hand synergy, **price leverage** (Δprice / price), **share lead vs rivals** in hand-strong names, meaningful capital deployed |
| Risk | Downside / fragility: wipeout proximity × size in that name, concentration, orphan low-price book without supportive cards, cash starvation |
| Low prices | Raise **both** chance and risk (e.g. 20 +60 ≈ ×4 leverage, but near wipeout). Strategies differ by how much potential must beat risk |
| Relative shares | Prefer holding **more shares than rivals** in companies the AI can move with its hand |
| Empty plays | Prefer Trade (or a rebalancing plan) when no card meaningfully improves chance / wealth / lead given current book |
| Anchors | Still include own net worth and lead terms so end ranking stays coherent; they must not drown risk/chance |
| Determinism | Pure function of public state + strategy (+ seeded RNG only where rules require it); stable tie-breaks |
| Lookahead | Keep bounded lookahead (no pile peeking); extend scoring inside that envelope — exact search shape in the plan |
| Weights | Exact numeric weights live in the **plan** (tuned to pass acceptance); requirements fix the *roles* of each term and the relative personality of the three profiles |

### Personality (weight roles)

| Profile | Feel | Chance | Risk | Wealth | Lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Aggressive** | Press leverage and share leads when the hand is live | high | low | medium | high |
| **Middle** | Judge risk vs potential (closest to the human play described) | medium | medium | high | medium |
| **Defensive** | Need clearer hand edge / smaller NW fraction before hot names | lower | high | high | low |

## Functional requirements

### Shared brain

1. All AI seats call the same chooser; only `strategy` selects the weight profile.
2. Candidate intents are scored with a shared feature vector at least including: wealth (or Δwealth), lead vs best rival, **chance**, **risk** (higher risk is worse).
3. **Chance** must reward: supportive cards × holdings, higher leverage at lower prices for a given upside card, and share lead (self − best rival) in those names.
4. **Risk** must penalize: wealth parked near wipeout without enough hand justification, extreme concentration / all-in fragility, and large low-price books with no supportive cards.
5. Buying at fair price may stay near wealth-neutral; **chance/risk** (and lead) are what make building a sensible book beat sitting on cash or yolo all-in.
6. Card play without a relevant position is disfavored unless it improves lead/chance enough under the profile weights (e.g. Aggressive crashing a rival’s lead name).

### Strategies & setup

7. Setup exposes Aggressive / Middle / Defensive (replace Investor / Raider / Mixed).
8. Default 4-seat table: 1 human + one AI of each profile (order documented in plan).
9. Same legal action space as humans; still deterministic; still no draw-pile cheating.

### Observable behavior (acceptance-level)

10. With cash and a hand that pumps company C, AI tends to **buy into C** (and contest share lead) rather than only playing C-cards while holding zero C.
11. At low prices, AI may buy when hand potential is strong (Aggressive more readily; Defensive more cautiously / smaller), and avoids dumping **most** of NW into a wipeout-edge name with a weak hand.
12. Middle differs from both extremes on at least one fixture where risk–potential tradeoff is the decider.
13. Same state + strategy ⇒ same intent sequence (seeded where needed).

## Non-functional requirements

- Browser-only; AI client-side; decisions bounded so the UI does not freeze
- Strategy scoring unit-testable without the DOM
- YAGNI: no extra strategies, no LLM, no speculative frameworks

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Strategies are Aggressive / Middle / Defensive in setup, seat chrome, and rules blurb
- [ ] Shared brain: one evaluation path; profiles are weights only
- [ ] Fixture: hand pumps C + cash → buys toward C / share lead instead of cash-only + empty plays
- [ ] Fixture: low price + strong hand → can take leveraged position (Aggressive clearest)
- [ ] Fixture: low price + weak hand / huge size → risk dominates (Defensive clearest; Aggressive still not pure suicidal all-in without edge)
- [ ] Fixture: Middle splits from Aggressive and Defensive on a risk-vs-potential choice
- [ ] Determinism test still holds
- [ ] Relevant engine tests green; short demo notes (1 human + 3 AI profiles, short rounds)

## Open questions

- Exact weight numbers and feature scaling (dollar-like vs 0–1) — decide in plan with tests as the contract.
- Whether Defensive gets any **hard** caps (e.g. max % NW in one name) vs soft risk only — prefer soft-first in plan unless tests need a hard floor.
- Default seat order for the three AI profiles at setup.
