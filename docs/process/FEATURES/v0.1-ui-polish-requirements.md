# Feature requirements: v0.1-ui-polish

**Status:** agreed  
**Owner:** Waldemar  
**Date:** 2026-08-15  

## Goal

Make the hotseat UI readable at a glance: whose turn it is, the current player’s hand always visible on their turn, relative share prices as a diagram, and buy/sell controls on each share row when trading is allowed.

## In scope

- Clear “whose turn” indicator for the current player
- Always show the current player’s hand cards while it is their turn (playable only in the play-card phase)
- Relative price diagram for the four companies (price-focused; no bank inventory UI)
- Buy and sell actions placed on each share/company row during trade phases
- Visual polish of the existing hotseat board
- Unlimited share supply (no bank stock / certificate limit)

## Out of scope

- Online multiplayer, new card types
- Price history / candlestick charts over time
- Showing other players’ hands

## Users / context

Hotseat players sharing one device who need to see turn state, their cards, and relative market levels without hunting through panels.

## Functional requirements

1. While `phase` is not `gameOver`, the UI clearly names the current player as “on turn.”
2. On the current player’s turn, their hand cards are always visible (title + text). Cards are interactive only in `chooseHandCard`; otherwise they are read-only.
3. Market prices are shown as a relative diagram (bar lengths scaled to the highest current price) with numeric price labels. The diagram does not show bank inventory.
4. When `canTrade(state)` is true, each company row in the market diagram exposes Buy and Sell for that company (using the shared quantity control), plus End turn.
5. Trade UI only when the phase allows trading. Buying is limited by cash only (and selling by holdings), not by any bank share pool.

## Non-functional requirements

- Browser-only; no new dependencies required for the chart (CSS/SVG)
- Keep the rules engine free of UI concerns; pure helpers for bar scaling are fine

## Acceptance checklist

Human reviews these outcomes (not code by default):

- [ ] Current player is obvious from the turn indicator
- [ ] Current player’s cards stay visible throughout their turn
- [ ] Relative price diagram makes company price levels comparable (no bank count on the diagram)
- [ ] Buy/Sell appear on share rows during Trade-only and optional post-Risk trade
- [ ] Cards are only playable when choosing a hand card
- [ ] Players can buy any quantity they can afford (no bank stock limit)
- [ ] Engine + relative-bar tests pass
- [ ] Demo via `npm run dev`
