# Feature requirements: v0.3-ai-opponents

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Waldemar  
**Date:** 2026-08-16  

## Goal

Extend Börsenspiel so games can run for a configurable number of rounds with recycled cards, and so any seat can be a deterministic (non-LLM) AI that plays with one of three named strategies chosen at setup.

## In scope

- Setup: per seat — human vs AI, and if AI which strategy
- Setup: **rounds** parameter (game length)
- Rule change: played cards return to the draw stack so the next draw stays random among remaining stack cards (see open questions for exact recycle mechanics)
- Three deterministic AI strategies (no LLM, no network calls)
- Auto-play on AI turns in hotseat (engine + UI wiring)
- Engine tests for recycle/end conditions and each strategy’s decision contract

## Out of scope

- Online multiplayer, accounts, persistence
- LLM / ML / learned policies
- Difficulty levels beyond the three strategies
- Showing AI “thinking” traces as a product feature (optional debug later)
- Changing the card catalog, split/wipeout math, or starting cash/hand sizes (except as required by rounds/recycle)

## Users / context

Hotseat players who want solo practice or mixed human/AI games on one device, with predictable AI behavior they can choose at setup.

## Functional requirements

### Setup

1. Keep 2–4 seats. Each seat has a name, **controller** (`human` | `ai`), and if `ai` a **strategy** (`wealth` | `punish` | `balanced` — final ids TBD in plan).
2. Add a **rounds** integer on setup (min/max/default TBD — see open questions).
3. Allow all-AI and mixed human/AI games. At least one seat required as today (2–4 total).

### Card recycle & game length

4. When a card is **played** (Action from hand or Risk resolved from draw), it is returned to the **draw stack** rather than sitting forever in a dead discard that never re-enters play. After return, the next draw is a **random** card from that stack (deterministic given the shuffle RNG seed used by the engine).
5. Game end is driven by **rounds**, not by emptying a non-recycling pile. Exact definition of one round is an open question (see below).
6. After the last allowed turn resolves (including optional post-Risk trade), score ranking as today: `cash + Σ(shares × price)`.

### AI (deterministic)

7. When `phase` requires a decision and the current seat is AI, the client/engine selects a single `Intent` (or a short fixed sequence for trade) via a pure function of `(state, strategy, rng?)` — **no LLM**.
8. AI must cover every decision point: Draw vs Trade-only; which hand card to play; `[?]` company choice; buy/sell/endTrade on trade phases.
9. **Strategy A — Wealth (`wealth`):** choose the legal action that best improves the AI’s own net worth (and, when comparing equals, a documented tie-break). Prefer evaluating immediate outcomes; multi-step lookahead depth is an open question.
10. **Strategy B — Punish (`punish`):** choose the legal action that most harms competitors relative to the AI (e.g. maximize own lead over the strongest rival, or minimize rivals’ aggregate wealth — exact objective fixed in plan).
11. **Strategy C — Balanced (`balanced`):** a fixed weighted mix of wealth and punish scores (weights documented in plan), not an LLM blend.
12. Tie-breaks are fully specified (e.g. prefer Draw over Trade, then lowest card id, then company order) so two runs with the same state + strategy + RNG stream yield the same intents.
13. Humans still play their seats as today; AI seats auto-advance without requiring a click (small UX delay optional — open question).

## Non-functional requirements

- Browser-only, no backend; AI runs entirely client-side
- Strategy modules unit-testable without the DOM
- Recycle + rounds changes covered by engine tests
- Performance: AI decision on a typical turn must feel instant on a laptop (no deep search that freezes the UI)

## Acceptance checklist

Human reviews these outcomes (not code by default):

- [ ] Setup lets each seat be human or AI with one of three strategies, plus a rounds setting
- [ ] Played cards re-enter the draw stack; subsequent draws are random from that stack
- [ ] Game ends after the configured rounds and shows the usual ranking
- [ ] An all-AI or mixed game can complete without human input on AI seats
- [ ] Wealth strategy visibly favors own net worth; Punish favors hurting rivals; Balanced sits between (spot-check + tests)
- [ ] Same state + strategy + seeded RNG → same AI intents (determinism test)
- [ ] Tests covering recycle, rounds end, and strategy selection exist and pass
- [ ] Demo / verification notes (e.g. `npm run dev`: 1 human + 1 AI, short rounds)

## Open questions

1. **What is one “round”?**  
   - (A) Every seat has taken one turn (full table cycle), game lasts N cycles  
   - (B) N turns total (across all players)  
   - (C) Something else you have in mind  

2. **Rounds default / bounds?** e.g. default 10 cycles, min 1, max 50?

3. **Recycle mechanics — which exact rule?**  
   - (A) After play, immediately insert the card at a **random position** in the remaining draw pile (no separate discard)  
   - (B) Push to discard; when draw pile would be empty (or after each play?), **shuffle discard back** into draw  
   - (C) After play, shuffle **that card + remaining draw pile** together  

4. **Do starting hand cards that get played also recycle into the draw pile?** (Almost certainly yes — confirm.)

5. **Does Risk drawn mid-turn recycle the same way as a played Action?**

6. **AI trade policy granularity:** may the AI buy/sell multiple companies in one trade phase, or one buy-or-sell then end? Any cap on share quantity steps (e.g. all-in / half / none)?

7. **Lookahead:** Wealth/Punish evaluate **one ply** (current decision only), or also “next steps” (e.g. 1–2 turns ahead with a simplified model of unknown draws)?

8. **AI turn UX:** instant auto-play, or ~300–800ms delay so humans can see what happened?

9. **Naming:** keep `wealth` / `punish` / `balanced`, or prefer player-facing labels (e.g. Investor / Raider / Mixed)?

## Proposed strategy definitions (for agreement)

| Id | Player-facing name | Objective (sketch) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `wealth` | Investor | Maximize own net worth after the decision; ties → maximize lead over #2 |
| `punish` | Raider | Maximize (own net worth − best rival net worth), preferring moves that drop rivals even if own wealth gains less |
| `balanced` | Mixed | Score = `0.5 * wealthScore + 0.5 * punishScore` (weights adjustable in plan) |

Card/choice evaluation can reuse the existing wealth-preview idea in `previewCardWealth` (extend for rivals / lead).
