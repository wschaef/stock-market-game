# Feature requirements: v0.6-ai-play-cards-bugfix

**Status:** agreed (bugfix; reproduces broken post–trader-brain behavior)  
**Owner:** Waldemar  
**Date:** 2026-08-26  

## Goal

Restore AI seats so they **Draw and play hand cards** after building positions, instead of Trade-only forever.

## In scope

- Fix choose-turn Draw scoring / realization so AI plays cards in normal games
- Keep idle-cash Trade-first when the hand pumps a name and cash is undeployed
- Regression tests for positioned → Draw and buy-then-draw/play

## Out of scope

- New strategies, catalog changes, UI redesign, LLM policies

## Functional requirements

1. From `chooseTurn`, Draw lookahead must actually simulate a legal hand play (not fail the phase check).
2. Idle cash + pump hand → still prefer Trade (buy into the name) over empty Draw.
3. After a matching book exists and cash is low / trade adds little wealth, AI prefers Draw and plays a card.
4. Multi-turn: after buying into a pump name, a later AI turn draws and plays.

## Acceptance checklist

- [x] Fixture: idle cash + pump hand → `startTrade`
- [x] Fixture: positioned + low cash → `draw`
- [x] Fixture: buy then later draw + `playCard`
- [x] Relevant engine tests green
