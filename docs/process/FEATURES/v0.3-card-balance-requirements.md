# Feature requirements: v0.3-card-balance

**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Waldemar  
**Date:** 2026-08-16  

## Goal

Rebalance +100 action cards into a player-assigned penalty layout, and lower how often Risk cards appear in the draw pile by putting more Action cards in the pile. Risk effect values stay as they are today.

## Deck inventory

Source: `src/engine/catalog.ts` / `docs/game-rules.md`. Total **63** cards (unchanged).

| Type | Count | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Small / standard** | **32** | ±30/40/50/60 with complementary `[?]` (90 − amount); 8 per magnitude |
| **Big +100** | **8** | Two identical copies per company; see §A |
| **Big ×2 / ½** | **8** | `2×` / `½` pairs per company |
| **Risk** | **15** | Fixed multi-company deltas; **effects unchanged** |

## In scope

- Redesign +100 cards and multi-company choice flow
- Change draw-pile mix to **9 Action + 3 Risk per player**
- Update rules, catalog, engine, UI, constants, and tests

## Out of scope

- Changing risk card delta values
- Changing standard (±30…60) or multiplier formulas
- Online multiplayer / AI / new companies

## Functional requirements

### A. +100 cards

1. Each card names one company that receives **+100**.
2. There are **8** such cards: **two identical copies** per company.
3. The player assigns **−10**, **−20**, and **−30** to the **other three** companies — one amount each (all different; never the named +100 company).
4. UI/engine: sequential picks showing which penalty is next; already-chosen companies unavailable.

### B. Draw-pile mix

| Constant | Was | New |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `ACTIONS_PER_PLAYER_IN_PILE` | 6 | **9** |
| `RISKS_PER_PLAYER_IN_PILE` | 3 | **3** |
| Risk deltas | — | **unchanged** |

When the pile fills completely: **P(draw is Risk) = 3/(9+3) = 25%** (was 33%).

| Players | Actions in hands | Actions in pile | Risks in pile | Pile size | P(draw is Risk) |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 8 | 18 | 6 | 24 | **25%** |
| 3 | 12 | 27 | 9 | 36 | **25%** |
| 4 | 16 | 32 (want 36) | 12 | 44 | **~27%** |

**4-player caveat:** only 48 action cards exist. After dealing 16 into hands, at most 32 remain for the pile (4 short of 36), so 4p lands at **~27%** unless we later add more action copies. Setup already takes as many remaining actions as exist. Risks unused stay 9 / 6 / 3 for 2p / 3p / 4p.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] `ACTIONS_PER_PLAYER_IN_PILE === 9`, `RISKS_PER_PLAYER_IN_PILE === 3`; rules text matches
- [ ] 2p/3p piles are exactly 9×n action + 3×n risk; 4p uses all 32 leftover actions + 12 risk
- [ ] Playing a +100 requires assigning −10/−20/−30 to three distinct other companies; duplicates rejected
- [ ] Prices/events after +100 match the assignment (including split/wipeout)
- [ ] Risk deltas unchanged from current catalog
- [ ] Tests cover multi-choice +100, pile mix, catalog length 63
- [ ] Demo: one +100 with three picks; setup/test shows new pile mix

## Open questions

- None for the chosen design. (4p ~27% risk rate is accepted as a consequence of a 48-action catalog.)
