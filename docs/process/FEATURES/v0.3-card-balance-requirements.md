**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Waldemar

## Goal

Rebalance the action/+100 and risk parts of the deck so +100 plays are skillful company assignment (not fixed blanket dumps), and risk draws are less swingy and/or less frequent.

**Draw-pile mix (unchanged today):** 6 Action + 3 Risk **per player** → risk share of the pile is always **⅓** (~33% of draws), leftover cards unused.

## In scope

- Redesign **+100** cards: named company **+100**; player assigns **−10**, **−20**, **−30** to the **three other** companies (all different — automatic with 3 others).
- Tune **risk**: reduce draw frequency and/or dampen effect sizes (choose option below).
- Update rules text, catalog, engine choice resolution, UI picker, and tests to match.

## Out of scope

- Changing standard (±30…60) or multiplier card formulas (unless needed as follow-up).
- Online multiplayer / AI.
- Adding new companies.

## Functional requirements

### A. +100 cards

1. Each +100 card names one company that receives **+100**.
2. The player must assign **−10**, **−20**, and **−30** to the **other three** companies — one amount each; **no duplicate company** (and not the named +100 company).
3. UI/engine: sequential company picks (or equivalent) showing which penalty is being assigned next; already-chosen companies are unavailable.
4. **Card count decision (pick one):**
   - **A1 (recommended):** **4** +100 cards (one per company). Removes the old −20 vs −30 duplicate pair; choice absorbs that differentiation. Action catalog becomes **44** (32 + 4 + 8); total deck **59**.
   - **A2:** Keep **8** +100 cards as **two identical copies** per company (preserve action density / old 48-action count).

### B. Risk probability & effects — options for approval

**Today:** mean |delta| ≈ **53**, max **±90**, mean per-card absolute swing ≈ **199**; pile risk rate **33%**.

| Option | Probability change | Effect change | Rough outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **R1** | `RISKS_PER_PLAYER_IN_PILE`: **3 → 2** (pile risk **25%**) | none | Fewer risk turns; same bombs when they hit |
| **R2** | keep 3 | scale all risk deltas by **⅔** (round to nearest 10) | max ~±60; mean |delta| ~35 |
| **R3** | keep 3 | scale by **½** (round to nearest 10) | max ~±50; softer overall |
| **R4 (recommended)** | **3 → 2** | scale by **⅔** + special-case soften extremes (cards 3, 13, 14, 15) | Less frequent **and** less violent |
| **R5** | **3 → 1** (pile risk ~14%) | scale by **⅔** | Rare risks, moderated when drawn |

**Recommended default if you want one package:** **A1 + R4**.
