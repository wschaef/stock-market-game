**Status:** draft  
**Owner:** Waldemar  
**Date:** 2026-08-16  

## Goal

Rebalance the action/+100 and risk parts of the deck so +100 plays are skillful company assignment (not fixed blanket dumps), and risk cards appear less often in the draw pile (effects unchanged).

## In scope

- Redesign **+100** cards: named company **+100**; player assigns **−10**, **−20**, **−30** to the **three other** companies (all different).
- Change draw-pile mix: **9 Action + 3 Risk per player** (was 6 + 3). Risk **effect values stay as today**.
- Update rules text, catalog, engine choice resolution, UI picker, constants, and tests.

## Out of scope

- Scaling or rewriting individual risk card deltas.
- Changing standard (±30…60) or multiplier card formulas.
- Online multiplayer / AI.
- Adding new companies.

## Functional requirements

### A. +100 cards

1. Each +100 card names one company that receives **+100**.
2. The player must assign **−10**, **−20**, and **−30** to the **other three** companies — one amount each; **no duplicate company** (and not the named +100 company).
3. UI/engine: sequential company picks (or equivalent) showing which penalty is being assigned next; already-chosen companies are unavailable.
4. **Card count (decided):** **A2** — **8** +100 cards as **two identical copies** per company. Action catalog stays **48**; total deck stays **63**.

### B. Draw-pile mix (decided)

| Constant | Was | New |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `ACTIONS_PER_PLAYER_IN_PILE` | 6 | **9** |
| `RISKS_PER_PLAYER_IN_PILE` | 3 | **3** (unchanged) |
| Risk card deltas | — | **unchanged** |

Ideal rate when the pile is fully filled: **3 / (9 + 3) = 25%** Risk on each Draw.

| Players | Actions in hands | Actions wanted in pile | Actions available after hands | Actions actually in pile | Risks in pile | Pile size | P(draw is Risk) |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 8 | 18 | 40 | **18** | 6 | 24 | **6/24 = 25%** |
| 3 | 12 | 27 | 36 | **27** | 9 | 36 | **9/36 = 25%** |
| 4 | 16 | 36 | 32 | **32** (short **4**) | 12 | 44 | **12/44 ≈ 27.3%** |

**4-player caveat:** only **48** action cards exist. Hands take 16, so at most **32** can enter the pile — not the ideal 36. Setup already takes “as many as remain”; risk rate for 4p becomes **~27%**, not 25%. Risks unused: 2p→9, 3p→6, 4p→3 (same as today).

**Resolution for 4p shortfall (pick one before implement):**

- **B1 (recommended):** Accept **~27%** for 4p; document it. No catalog growth.
- **B2:** Use **8** actions/player instead of 9 → exact fill for all player counts; rate **3/(8+3) ≈ 27.3%** always.
- **B3:** Add **4** more action copies to the catalog so 4p can fill 36 + keep true **25%**.

### C. Risk effects

No change. Keep current ± magnitudes (including ±80/±90 cards).
