# Feature requirements: v0.5-action-log

**Status:** agreed  
**Owner:** Waldemar  
**Date:** 2026-08-17  

## Goal

Make the action log a readable record of what happened: trades of the same share sit on one line with price and cash total, played cards name the chosen company instead of `[?]`, and Risk draws list their real effects.

## In scope

- Action-log text produced by the engine (`GameState.log`)
- Last-drawn Risk banner copy (same effect summary as the log)
- Risk card face: show the card’s title (`Risk N`) instead of a generic “risk” badge

## Out of scope

- Changing trade rules, prices, or card ops
- Redesigning log layout beyond readable wrapping of longer lines
- Naming a drawn Action before it is played (privacy from v0.4 stays)

## Users / context

Players (and spectators of AI turns) use the action log to reconstruct the last trades and card resolutions. Today buy/sell spam separate lines, `[?]` is never filled in, and “resolves Risk: Risk 1” hides the actual market move.

## Functional requirements

1. **Same-share trades merge.** During one trade session, buy and/or sell of the same company by the current player update a **single** log line. Trades of different companies stay on separate lines. A later turn starts a new line.
2. **Price and total.** A buy or sell line includes the per-share price and the cash total for that side (both sides when the line has buys and sells).
3. **Resolved `[?]`.** After a company is chosen, the play line names that company. Intermediate “chooses X for `[?]`” lines are not added. Unbound choices may still show `[?]` until picked. A fully bound play never contains `[?]`.
4. **Risk output.** Drawing a Risk is one log line: actor, risk title, and each company effect (signed delta plus resulting price; split/wipeout called out). The last-drawn banner uses the same effect summary. The Risk card face shows `Risk N` (the catalog title).
5. **Action draw privacy.** Drawing an Action still must not put that card’s title in the log; the play line (with resolved companies) is public.

## Non-functional requirements

- Formatters are pure and unit-tested (TDD)
- Engine remains the source of log text (UI only displays it)
- No new runtime dependencies

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Buy then sell (or two buys) of the same share in one trade session appear as one log line with price and totals
- [ ] Buy of A and sell of B remain two lines
- [ ] Playing a card with `[?]` logs the chosen company on the play line; no leftover `?` after the last pick
- [ ] Drawn Action titles stay out of the log until play
- [ ] Drawn Risk log line lists company deltas (and split/wipeout when they happen)
- [ ] Last-drawn Risk banner includes the effect summary; Risk card shows `Risk N`
- [ ] Tests for the formatters and reduce() log behavior pass
